---
name: Diary entry interaction
description: User-requested interaction convention for Happy Colon daily entry tiles and bowel logging.
---

Daily entry tiles in Diary should open their details from the bottom as a sheet. Do not expand their details below the grid. Bowel logging should be available directly from its Diary tile, with the same current-day record visible in Calendar.

**Why:** The user specifically rejected inline details because they require scrolling to reach the controls, and rejected routing to Calendar just to log a bowel movement.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing Diary entry actions, keep the tile grid compact, put detailed controls in bottom sheets, and use the existing shared bowel record rather than separate Diary-only data.
